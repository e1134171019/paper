# Evidence-Family Dedup Method v2

## Purpose

Prevent multiple papers from the same research program from inflating the count of independent experimental evidence.

## Rule

A shared surname or one overlapping coauthor is not sufficient to collapse two records into one evidence family. Conversely, different paper titles or topology labels are not sufficient to grant independent-family credit.

Assess four dimensions:

1. **Lead/corresponding role overlap** — strongest signal when the same person is sole author, corresponding author, or methodology lead.
2. **Core coauthor overlap** — repeated central author group is stronger than one incidental/historical coauthor.
3. **Topology lineage** — explicit modified/derived/trans-inverse/quadratic lineage from the same research program reduces independence.
4. **Prototype/program continuity** — shared laboratory platform or clear sequential paper series reduces independence when source-supported.
