## Policy

Independent-family credit is not assigned by paper count or surname count. The decision considers corresponding/lead-author role, methodology ownership, topology lineage, recurring core-author overlap, experiment/research program, and whether the new record is a genuinely independent architecture/program rather than an incremental topology variant.
